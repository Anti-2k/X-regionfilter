When X only gives a region

X does not always report a country. Often it reports only a subregion — “Eastern Europe”, “the European Union”, “Southeast Asia” — with no country name at all. If you blocked Russia and an account reports Eastern Europe, that account may or may not be the thing you blocked, and no amount of guessing changes that. So it is your call:

A region that is fully inside your list is never ambiguous — blocking every country in Eastern Europe hides accounts reported as “Eastern Europe” too. Likewise blocking a whole region hides every country inside it.
