Where locations come from

Both come from X’s “About this account” page. X allows about 50 lookups every 15 minutes, so results are cached for 30 days and the filter gets more complete the more you browse. Posts whose author hasn’t been looked up yet follow “No location” below.

Turn both off and no lookups are made; only your fixed locations below apply.
